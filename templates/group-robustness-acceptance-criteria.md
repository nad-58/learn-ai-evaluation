# Group and Robustness Acceptance Criteria

Acceptance criteria should be defined for the specific system and use context. The entries below are prompts, not universal thresholds.

## Decision categories

- **Pass:** predefined performance and evidence criteria are met.
- **Review:** a criterion is not met or evidence is insufficient; investigate before making a conclusion.
- **Fail:** a material predefined limit is exceeded and the system should not proceed without mitigation.
- **Not evaluable:** data, class coverage, or analysis design does not support a valid estimate.
